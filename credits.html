PIIK KENYA · PRE-LAUNCH

Image credits & source status

This review preview makes the status of its sources explicit. No stock photograph has been copied from the design references.

PIIK identity

The original PIIK logo is retained in assets/logo.png. The smaller WebP display version is trimmed and resized from that file, preserving the original blue artwork and wording. PIIK’s green, gold and ivory website palette surrounds the logo without altering the original mark.

Nairobi skyline — review use only

The skyline comes from assets/hero-nairobi.jpg, already present in the supplied repository. The preview uses an optimised WebP derivative, assets/nairobi-preview.webp. The repository contains no photographer attribution or licence record, and external image-source access is blocked in this environment.

Usage rights have not been verified. The image is labelled accordingly in the preview. Do not release this redesign publicly with this image until PIIK confirms the source and appropriate rights or replaces it with a documented, licensed Kenyan property or Nairobi image. Existing repository presence is not evidence of a licence.

Reference screenshots

The supplied references informed the forest-green background, gold accents, rounded imagery and clear calls to action. Their watermarked property photograph has not been extracted, copied or reused.

Other artwork and fonts

The interface icons, favicon and diaspora globe composition were drawn for this implementation using simple SVG geometry and CSS. They use no third-party icon library. Georgia and Arial are selected as locally available system fonts, with system fallbacks; no fonts are downloaded.

Official resource status

EARB, Ardhisasa, the Law Society of Kenya, the Land Surveyors’ Board and the Business Registration Service are described as resources to consult, with no claimed affiliation. Outbound requests to the candidate official websites were blocked by the environment’s network policy on 17 November 2026. Direct links are therefore withheld rather than presented as verified.

Before adding links, confirm each body’s identity, HTTPS destination and redirects, and the relevant register or service using official first-party evidence. Check reachability separately from whether a search service is usable or requires an account. Record the URL, check date and any access conditions in the project’s source register.
