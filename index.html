02 / OFFICIAL VERIFICATION HUB

Go to the source.

Know which official body to consult and what its checks can tell you. PIIK has no formal partnerships with these organisations.

Use the authority relevant to your question. Linked destinations were supplied through the founder’s independent research. Published registers and information pages are distinguished below; they are not all live status-checking services. Check availability, fees and current status with the authority.

01 / PUBLISHED 2026 REGISTER

EARB

Estate Agents Registration Board

Read EARB’s publication of the 2026 Kenya Gazette register of estate agents and estate-agent companies. It provides evidence of entries covered by that published register.

This is a dated publication, not a live practising-status check. Confirm later changes and current requirements with EARB. An entry does not establish authority over a particular property.

02 / LAND SERVICES PORTAL

Ardhisasa

Digital land services

Access the official land-services portal for supported applications and searches. Use the appropriate official process to obtain property-specific records.

Services, account requirements and coverage depend on the property and registry. Opening the portal or viewing a title copy does not confirm ownership or transaction safety.

04 / PUBLISHED REGISTER

Land Surveyors’ Board

Licensed land surveyors

Consult the Land Surveyors’ Board’s register for listed land surveyors and the registration information it publishes. Match the person’s full details.

Treat the listing as a published register unless the Board explicitly confirms a live status service. Check the register date and current licensing or practising position with the Board. A listing does not establish parcel boundaries.

05 / BUSINESS SERVICES GATEWAY

BRS

Business Registration Service

Find the official route to business and company registration services and the relevant records or searches. Use the actual service result for the entity concerned.

The homepage is a gateway, not a company-search result. Account requirements and fees may apply. Business registration does not establish land ownership, project approval, solvency or investment safety.

VRB

Valuers Registration Board

Consult the Valuers Registration Board for registration information and the route to checking a valuer’s current practising requirements.

The homepage is not an individual status result or a property valuation. Confirm the person’s details and current status with the Board, and commission property-specific valuation work separately.

Architects & quantity surveyors

Consult BORAQS’ separate published registers for architects and quantity surveyors. Match the professional’s name, discipline and registration details.

A published register is not automatically a live practising-status service. Check the publication date and current practising requirements with BORAQS. Registration does not confirm project planning or building approval.

04 / DIASPORA PROPERTY HUB

Far from home.
Closer to the right information.

Distance can make property decisions harder. Build an independent support team in Kenya and keep control of the process.

  1. 01

    Appoint independent expertise

    Choose your own advocate and relevant licensed professionals. Confirm their standing independently, rather than relying only on a seller’s referral.

  2. 02

    Confirm the property and authority

  3. 03

05 / KNOWLEDGE CENTRE

Clarity before commitment.

Practical reading for the questions that matter. Open a guide to get started.

Practical starting points for discussion with independent professionals. These guides are educational starting points; confirm current requirements with the relevant authority and your independent advisers. Terms of Use.

Land recordsWhat a land search can—and cannot—tell you+

Ask an independent advocate to identify the parcel, relevant registry and current official search route. Ardhisasa supports some land services; coverage and the correct process must be confirmed for the property.

Reconcile the official record with seller identity, authority, restrictions, charges and required consents. Ask a licensed land surveyor to assess boundaries, access and whether the site matches the parcel. A search and a site assessment answer different questions.

Repeat time-sensitive checks as your advocate advises before completion. Retain the official result and its date; do not rely solely on a photograph of a title or a forwarded search.

Relevant authorities: Ardhisasa · Land Surveyors’ Board · LSK

Estate agentsChoose the person as carefully as the property+

Obtain the agent’s full practising name, business details and registration information. Ask EARB about current registration and practising requirements, and confirm the identity matches the person you are engaging.

Have an independent advocate examine the seller’s or landlord’s authority and the agent’s written mandate for that specific property. Agree services, fees, conflicts and refund terms in writing.

Call a separately obtained number to confirm payment details. Company registration, a branded office or a social media profile cannot replace these checks.

Relevant authorities: EARB · BRS · LSK

DevelopersSeparate the company, the land and the project+

Identify the contracting legal entity and use the relevant BRS service to investigate its records. Ask who owns the land and whether that entity has the authority to develop and sell.

Ask independent advisers to check applicable county planning/building permissions, environmental requirements, contractor status and project registration. Compare approval references with the actual site, plans and developer.

Inspect past delivery claims independently. Ask about funding, construction oversight, utilities, access, completion responsibilities and ongoing management costs. Registration is one check, not proof of delivery.

Relevant authorities: BRS · Ardhisasa · Development approvals · NCA · NEMA

Off-plan purchasesUnderstand what happens if delivery changes+

Have your own advocate review the proposed agreement before paying a reservation fee. Establish what is being sold, the land interest, specifications, price, taxes, fees and completion conditions.

Ask about payment milestones, evidence of progress, changes to plans, delay remedies, cancellation, refund terms and what happens if the developer cannot complete. Agree safeguards with your adviser; do not assume a payment is protected.

Use an independent professional for progress reports where appropriate. Show homes, architectural illustrations and promised returns do not establish that the contracted home will be delivered.

Relevant authorities: LSK · BRS · Development approvals · BORAQS

Fraud awarenessPause when the story or payment details change+

Warning signs include urgency, refusal of independent checks, inconsistent names, altered payment instructions and requests to keep a transaction secret. These are reasons to pause and investigate, not public findings of wrongdoing.

Keep messages, agreements and receipts securely. Verify identities and account instructions through an independently obtained contact rather than a number included in a suspicious message. Never share PINs, passwords or one-time codes.

If money has already moved and you suspect fraud, promptly contact your bank or payment provider and seek independent legal advice about reporting and preservation of evidence. Do not publish accusations or send sensitive records to PIIK.

Relevant authorities: LSK · EARB · BRS

Independent professionalsBuild a team with clear roles+

An advocate can advise on the legal transaction; a land surveyor on parcel and boundary matters; a valuer on valuation; an architect, quantity surveyor or engineer on the relevant design, cost or engineering scope. Define the work you need before appointing someone.

Confirm current registration and practising status with the relevant body. Ask about experience, conflicts of interest, who will perform the work and how concerns are handled.

Agree scope, written deliverables, fees, timelines and reporting directly. A seller’s referral may be useful, but choose advisers who can act independently for your interests.

Relevant authorities: LSK · Land Surveyors’ Board · VRB · BORAQS · EBK

Diaspora planningKeep control of a property process from abroad+

Choose your own advocate and independent site representative in Kenya. Agree how you will verify identities, receive reports and confirm instructions across time zones. Do not delegate every check to the person selling the property.

Ask your advocate about the legal scope, execution and registration requirements of any power of attorney before appointing a representative. Share only the authority required for the task and keep a record of instructions.

Budget for professional fees, taxes, currency conversion and transfer costs. Confirm payment recipients independently, plan milestone reviews and arrange secure document handling with your advisers. Video tours supplement independent site checks; they do not replace them.

Before travelling or paying, work through the property checklist and prepare questions about access, occupancy, utilities, title, approvals and future management. Keep an organised record that your independent advisers can review.

Relevant authorities: LSK · Ardhisasa · Land Surveyors’ Board · Development approvals
