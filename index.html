04 / DIASPORA PROPERTY HUB

Far from home.
Closer to the right information.

Distance can make property decisions harder. Build an independent support team in Kenya and keep control of the process.

  1. 01

    Appoint independent expertise

    Choose your own advocate and relevant licensed professionals. Confirm their standing independently, rather than relying only on a seller’s referral.

  2. 02

    Confirm the property and authority

    Ask your advocate about official searches, site checks and approvals. If someone acts for you, discuss the scope and safeguards of any power of attorney.

  3. 03

    Plan payment and follow-up

    Understand the agreement, milestones, taxes and payment safeguards before sending funds. A video tour or family introduction is not an official check.

05 / KNOWLEDGE CENTRE

Clarity before commitment.

Practical reading for the questions that matter. Open a guide to get started.

Fraud awarenessSpot pressure before it becomes a loss+

Be cautious about urgency, guaranteed returns and unusually low prices. Independently confirm identities and authority to act. Do not rely on screenshots, social media badges or forwarded documents. If details conflict, pause and speak to an independent advocate before transferring money.

General education, not legal advice or a finding about any property or person.

Credential checksA professional title is only a starting point+

Ask for the professional’s full practising name and relevant registration details. Use the appropriate official register or contact the responsible body independently. Confirm current status and the scope of their work; a listing does not guarantee conduct or transaction safety.

General education, not legal advice or a finding about any property or person.

Your commitment checklistBefore any money changes hands+

Ask an independent advocate to explain the agreement, official searches and payment safeguards. Confirm the property details, seller’s authority, fees and refund terms. Verify payment instructions using a separately obtained contact. Keep your own records and never share PINs, passwords or one-time codes.

General education, not legal advice or a finding about any property or person.

ABOUT PIIK KENYA

Connecting property.
Building trust.

Property Integrity Institute – Kenya is an early-stage, independent property integrity initiative. Our mission is to improve trust, transparency and accountability by connecting consumers, property professionals and official verification resources.

We are in the pre-launch validation stage: learning what people need, building public confidence and exploring a professional network. All initial PIIK services are free.

PIIK is not a statutory regulator, licensing authority, estate agency or legal adviser. We do not certify professionals, guarantee transactions or establish that a property is safe to buy.
