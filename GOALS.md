# Project 003 goals

PIIK Kenya helps Kenyan residents, diaspora communities, property purchasers, renters and professionals find official resources and make informed decisions. The five services are Check for official verification resources, Directory for business discovery, Weekly for property intelligence, Watch for notices and consumer awareness, and Knowledge for education.

PIIK publishes independent information. Government bodies and qualified independent advisers provide official records and transaction-specific advice. PIIK offers no brokerage, certification, safety guarantee or commercial transaction service.

Preserve the site’s accessible, lightweight design. Automate collection, validation and reporting where evidence and access allow. Reserve editorial judgement for review. Keep the founder’s work within two hours a week and the executive report readable in 15 minutes. Use existing infrastructure with zero additional operating cost.
