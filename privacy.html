Privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

No online enquiry collection

The website currently has no active enquiry or expression-of-interest form. Disabled status cards do not register you. We do not request uploads, identity documents, title deeds, bank details or payment information.

Checklists and measurement

Your checklist selections remain in the current page’s memory and clear on reload. They are not sent to PIIK or an official service. The website sets no cookies, writes no browser storage and runs no analytics, advertising trackers or third-party scripts. A future measurement plan has been prepared but is inactive.

Hosting and external services

Website hosting may process ordinary technical information, such as IP addresses, user agents and request logs, to deliver and secure the site. PIIK does not claim that hosting logs are anonymous. Any future official-resource links lead to independently operated services with their own privacy terms; those services may require an account or payment.

Before enquiries open

The founder must approve the provider, destination mailbox and collection notice. The notice must identify the data controller and contact, purpose and lawful basis, required and optional fields, recipients/processors, retention and deletion rules, security, data rights and any cross-border processing safeguards. Marketing requires a separate, optional decision. Collection remains disabled until these arrangements and end-to-end delivery are tested.

Privacy questions

The existing contact is info@piikkenya.com. Mailbox monitoring must be confirmed before enquiries open. Do not send sensitive documents. See the Terms of Use for the scope of our guidance.
