PIIK KENYA · PRE-LAUNCH

Privacy & service limitations

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This information describes the pre-launch website. PIIK is validating demand; online interest registration is not open.

Online enquiries and registration are unavailable

This pre-launch website does not accept online enquiries or professional registrations. The two status cards have disabled buttons and no personal-information fields. There is no working submission endpoint and no information is sent from these sections. Viewing a card does not register you, request a service or create a membership.

Keep sensitive information private

Please do not send passports, national identity documents, title deeds, bank details, passwords or other sensitive records. The website provides no document-upload facility.

Checklists, cookies and hosting

Checklist selections stay in the current page and are not sent to a verification service. This site’s code sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising trackers or browser storage. Fonts, scripts, styles and images are served locally with the website.

The hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information, such as IP addresses, user agents and access logs, to operate and secure the website. Browser software and hosting infrastructure operate separately from the website’s guidance features.

External resources

Official-resource links are withheld while awaiting verification. If links are added later, visiting an external service will be subject to that service’s own terms, charges and privacy practices. PIIK does not operate official registers and has no formal partnerships with the listed bodies.

What PIIK does and does not do

PIIK is an independent, early-stage property integrity initiative. All initial PIIK services are free. Guidance is educational; it does not establish ownership, authenticity, regulatory status, suitability or transaction safety. Marking checklist steps as reviewed is not a verification result.

PIIK is not a statutory regulator, licensing authority, estate agency or legal adviser. No membership, partnership, accreditation, certification or endorsement is conferred by using this site. Engage independent qualified professionals and consult the relevant official bodies before committing funds.

Before real registration opens

PIIK must configure and test a submission provider and publish appropriate collection information before receiving responses. This must explain who controls the data, the specific purpose and lawful basis, required and optional fields, consent for any follow-up, recipients and processors, retention, any cross-border processing, security and how to exercise data rights. No marketing consent is collected by this website.

Privacy enquiries can be addressed to the contact shown on the existing PIIK site: info@piikkenya.com. Mailbox monitoring and readiness must be confirmed before a live collection service is launched. Please do not send sensitive documents.
