Privacy & service limitations

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This information describes the Phase 1 review preview. PIIK is validating demand; online interest registration is not open.

Your response stays in this page

The consumer and professional forms are local response previews only. Their contents are not sent to PIIK, Formspree or another submission provider, and are not stored in browser storage. Reviewing a response does not register you, request a service or create a membership.

Entries exist in the current page’s memory. Use “Clear my entries” to remove them. Reloading or leaving the page normally clears them, although browser autofill, back/forward restoration, extensions or a shared-device user may retain or see information. Avoid entering personal data on a shared device.

Keep sensitive information out

Please do not enter passport or national ID numbers, title deeds, bank details, exact property addresses, confidential client information or other sensitive records. There are no upload fields. Names, email and general locations are optional in this preview; use a country or county rather than a street address. Do not name or make allegations about third parties in free text.

Checklists, cookies and hosting

Checklist selections stay in the current page and are not sent to a verification service. This site’s code sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising trackers or browser storage. Fonts, scripts, styles and images are served locally with the website.

The hosting provider may process ordinary technical request information, such as IP addresses, user agents and access logs, to operate and secure the website. Browser software and hosting infrastructure operate separately from the page’s local-only form behaviour.

External resources

Official-resource links in this preview are withheld while awaiting verification. If links are added later, visiting an external service will be subject to that service’s own terms, charges and privacy practices. PIIK does not operate official registers and has no formal partnerships with the listed bodies.

What PIIK does and does not do

PIIK is an independent, early-stage property integrity initiative. All initial PIIK services are free. Guidance is educational; it does not establish ownership, authenticity, regulatory status, suitability or transaction safety. Marking checklist steps as reviewed is not a verification result.

PIIK is not a statutory regulator, licensing authority, estate agency or legal adviser. No membership, partnership, accreditation, certification or endorsement is conferred by using this site. Engage independent qualified professionals and consult the relevant official bodies before committing funds.

Before real registration opens

PIIK must configure and test a submission provider and publish appropriate collection information before receiving responses. This must explain who controls the data, the specific purpose and lawful basis, required and optional fields, consent for any follow-up, recipients and processors, retention, any cross-border processing, security and how to exercise data rights. Current preview acknowledgements are not consent to marketing.

Privacy enquiries can be addressed to the contact shown on the existing PIIK site: info@piikkenya.com. Mailbox monitoring and readiness must be confirmed before a live collection service is launched. Please do not send sensitive documents.
