04 / DIASPORA PROPERTY HUB

Far from home.
Closer to the right information.

Distance can make property decisions harder. Build an independent support team in Kenya and keep control of the process.

  1. 01

    Appoint independent expertise

    Choose your own advocate and relevant licensed professionals. Confirm their standing independently, rather than relying only on a seller’s referral.

  2. 02

    Confirm the property and authority

    Ask your advocate about official searches, site checks and approvals. If someone acts for you, discuss the scope and safeguards of any power of attorney.

  3. 03

    Plan payment and follow-up

    Understand the agreement, milestones, taxes and payment safeguards before sending funds. Agree independent progress reports and keep copies of receipts. Confirm changed payment details by calling a separately verified number.
