ABOUT PIIK KENYA

Connecting property.
Building trust.

Property Integrity Institute – Kenya is an early-stage, independent property integrity initiative. Our mission is to improve trust, transparency and accountability by connecting consumers, property professionals and official verification resources.

Our website is live, with free guidance for consumers and the diaspora. We are developing a multidisciplinary professional network around clear expectations, independent information and practical connections.

Learn about our scope in the Terms of Use and how we handle information in our Privacy notice.

03 / THE PROFESSIONAL NETWORK

Better connections.
Higher expectations.

A stronger property sector starts with people willing to work transparently.

We’re developing a proposed professional network for estate agents, advocates, land surveyors, valuers, architects, quantity surveyors, engineers, developers and property managers. The aim is to connect complementary expertise with people who need it, with clear roles and transparent fees.
